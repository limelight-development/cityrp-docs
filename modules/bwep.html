    Sharedbwep.modelOffsetsSource ☍
    Sharedbwep.offsetsSource ☍

    Slots 1-5 are local to the chest attachment and 6-7 to the pelvis, but both frames are player aligned: -X is behind the body, +Y is the player's left, -Z is down. See bwep.getSlotAnchor.

    Angles read the same way in either. Yaw 270 with roll 0 lays a gun flat against the back with its right flank facing out, and the pitch then swings the muzzle from the player's right (0) round to straight down (90) without lifting it off the body -- that is the family slots 4 and 5 sit in, shallow across the kidneys and steep across the shoulder blades respectively. Slots 6-7 hang a pistol muzzle down beside the leg, canted a few degrees so the grip leads the way a belt holster does.

    Only slots 1-3 pose differently depending on what the player is holding. 4-7 declare Normal alone and drawPlayerWeapons falls back to it.

    Sharedbwep.HIP_BONESource ☍

    Bone the hip slots hang from. The chest attachment sits at the top of the spine, so a hip measured down from it is out by however long the torso of the player model happens to be, and drifts off the body the moment an animation moves the chest. The pelvis sits between the hip joints on every ValveBiped model and travels with the legs, which is what a belt holster does.
