cityrp.chatBox.getInputBackgroundSource ☍

Backdrop colour for the chat input bar and the filter box. Follows the log panel's tint and opacity so the two read as one surface. The bar was fixed at black 150 regardless of the setting, so raising the opacity or tinting the log left the bar a visibly different shade to the panel sitting directly above it.

Only once the panel is actually switched on, though. With it off there is nothing above the bar to match, and tinting the bar on its own would just recolour a surface the player never asked to change.

Returns:

  1. Color The colour to fill the bar with.
cityrp.chatBox.getInputOffsetSource ☍

How far below the chat position the input bar sits. getPosition has to know this to keep the bar clear of the HUD, so it is shared rather than repeated. Gap between the chat position and the top of the input bar. Derived from the line spacing rather than fixed, so it keeps pace with the chat text size. The newest message's baseline sits barely above the chat position, and a flat 6px left the bar sitting on top of that last line.

Returns:

  1. number offset in pixels.
