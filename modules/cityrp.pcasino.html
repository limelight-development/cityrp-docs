cityrp.pcasino.AutoExcludeTiersSource ☍

Ascending net-loss tiers that trigger automatic, escalating self-exclusion. Each entry's duration is played-time seconds the exclusion lasts. The loss tally is NOT reset when a tier fires, so a player who keeps playing once an exclusion lifts escalates straight to the next tier rather than restarting.

cityrp.pcasino.BoundPrizeItemsSource ☍

Item uniqueIDs that receive hidden tradable=false meta when granted by the casino. Already definition-locked prizes (M3, Ninja, Dragon's Breath, Golden Vape) are omitted. Trabbi is intentionally left tradable as the joke prize.

cityrp.pcasino.LossWarningTiersSource ☍

Ascending net-loss tiers (RP cash, this sitting) that trigger an in-game reminder only. Each tier notifies the player once per session; the last tier also pings staff.
